# Music

`lantern-gospel-04` is an original 32-second, wordless sacred choir. Its D-major centre, added sixths and ninths, common-tone voice leading and soft G-to-D plagal return favour warmth over threat. One relative-minor phrase adds shade. The earlier Phrygian semitone, altered dominant and descending dark bass have been removed. The design is a spacious festival surrounding one solitary passenger, without a march or drum groove.

Six parts contain four separately synthesized singers each. A differentiated glottal-flow cycle excites five vocal-tract resonances per singer. Bass, tenor and alto vowel parameters differ; individual tract length, pitch drift, vibrato and breathing also differ. The vowel gradually opens from O toward A as the choir swells. These are synthetic vowel voices, not recordings or intelligible Latin lyrics.

The two sides and three tiers of swans map to the six voice groups. Their wing and neck gestures use the same section envelopes as the corresponding audio voices. The duck's small calls stay close to the stereo centre; the massed choir occupies the sides and the reverberant hall. A reduced low pedal and quiet, consonant bells provide scale without a drum groove.

Persistent oscillators receive a sub-cent integrated-phase correction. Three laps are rendered with a finite 7.2-second room response, then the settled middle lap is retained. Pausing and seeking use the same scene clock as the visuals.
